# Grimsby Institute: creative and implementation brief

## Guiding direction
Bespoke visual craftsmanship is the highest priority. A lavishly typeset Victorian institutional publication, with theatrical detail and genuinely comfortable mobile reading. The Institute treats itself seriously; the disproportionate attention given to ridiculous details communicates the creator's humor. No generic card-grid homepage, modern dashboard styling, skull wallpaper, or jokes that announce themselves.

## Identity
Warm ivory, near-black ink, oxblood accents, engraved illustration, custom Baryon-and-rat heraldry, ornamental rules. Autumn decoration is a separate physical-looking layer: mourning fabric, dried foliage, and rust-colored ribbon. The building has pumpkins at its gates. No definitive likeness of H-31 and no invented reason for that uncertainty.

## Story
H-31 is front and center. Entries I–IX launch together. Preserve their original headings, wording, punctuation, and line breaks; replace only the placeholder dates with deliberately strange dates. Each entry has its own real page, previous/next navigation, and stable URL. The archive has a chronological index; the homepage links to the beginning and latest dispatch.

Grimsby is en route to Maine at the end of IX, not already arrived. H-31 was reported in Maine. The map distinguishes Grimsby's journey from reported subject sightings and gains new legs only as the story advances. The Institute is located in “Vaguely England”; no exact city is invented.

Journals establish context behind the dinner without explaining the future courses. No menu or future itinerary spoilers. Archive titles are navigation labels; original journal headings remain on their own pages.

## Visitor information
October 24, 2026, 7 p.m.; Covington, Kentucky only. Costumes not necessary. Exact address shared privately. Spoken door phrase: “Today belongs to the rat.” No web authentication. RSVP asks guests to contact their host until a real form destination is supplied.

## Publishing
Use the existing Collective001/GrimsbyInstitute repository and GitHub Pages. Replaces the previous attempt; prior source remains in Git history. Build static HTML so entries load independently, deep links work, and updates do not depend on a framework or database.
