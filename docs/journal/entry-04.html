Entry IV

The Journal of Elias Grimsby

Wednesday, by Laboratory Reckoning

Subject H-31 grows more animated.

It is hard to gauge if this behavior is curiosity or agitation.

Thankfully a Reese Confection continues to remedy this behavior.

I have asked the institute custodian, Jeff, to remove the orange and black taffy from the premises.

I find it doubtful that Jeff will follow my instructions fully, though.

I informed Jeff to remove them, take them outside...

burn them...

then put out the fire with the posterior end of Dr. Melster.

I will settle for the taffy being removed.

Subject H-31 has continued observing the birds as they fly by.

If Subject H-31 continues this behavior and a hunting cap becomes involved...

... termination of experiment will happen post-haste.

Observations shall continue.

Signed Elias Grimsby Grimsby Institute
