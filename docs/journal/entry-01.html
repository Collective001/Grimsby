Entry I

The Journal of Elias Grimsby

The Third Monday of October

It has come to my attention that a written record of my research is required.

I am fulfilling this demand...

Research concerning Subject C-25 has not progressed as much as I would have preferred.

The subject's insistence upon serving as a behavioral chaperone is becoming bothersome.

I would prefer to conduct my work without Subject C-25 constantly evaluating me.

The subject is...

relentlessly observant.

I am growing increasingly convinced that Subject C-25 has developed some manner of psychic ability.

Whether I am awake or sleeping...

Subject C-25 seems to be aware.

More troublingly, it appears to have formed opinions regarding my behavior.

I have found no satisfactory explanation for this.

Suggested action: Shelve Subject C-25 until a later date.

A long nap may relax the subject...

and would permit me to work without supervision.

For now...

Subject H-31 shows considerably more promise.

H-31 appears decidedly more agreeable, provided sweets are offered in recompense.

The response has been remarkably consistent.

No sweets...

little cooperation.

Sweets...

considerably more cooperation.

Further study is required, of course.

However...

I find myself cautiously optimistic.

Subject H-31 may prove to be the success story this Institute needs.

Signed, Elias Grimsby Grimsby Institute
