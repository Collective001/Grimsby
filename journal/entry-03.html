Entry III

The Journal of Elias Grimsby

The Thirty-Second of September

Progress has been made with Subject H-31.

It seems H-31 has...

... preferences.

When offered a sweet from Reese Laboratory, Subject H-31 responds with enthusiasm.

Though...

... when offered a peanut taffy, Subject H-31 recoils.

To be thorough I tested H-31 with both the orange ones and then with the black ones.

This fits my larger hypothesis that no creature enjoys the orange and black taffy.

The confection industry shall be shaken with this finding.

Though I feel future generations will be thankful for these findings.

Subject H-31 seems to have generated an affinity for the laboratory windows as of late.

All goes well, Subject H-31 and I shall explore the institute grounds.

My next move is to inform Dr. Melster that his orange and black confections are an abomination.

Perhaps I shall word it differently...

... perhaps I won't

Signed Elias Grimsby Grimsby Institute
