Entry V

The Journal of Elias Grimsby

The Morning Immediately Following a Thursday

It appears that, in my haste yesterday, I absent-mindedly neglected to lock Subject H-31's enclosure.

Mistakes were made.

Questions have been answered.

I can now conclude that the structural integrity of the subject appears to be superior...

... to that of the laboratory window.

It is safe to conclude that Subject H-31 was not interested in birds...

... Subject H-31 was learning how to fly.

I am also moved to the conclusion that Subject H-31 was more curious than agitated.

This finding is favorable.

I must also conclude that Subject H-31 has flown off with what was going to be my breakfast.

This finding is unfavorable.

Future journals shall be from outside of the institute.

Signed Elias Grimsby Grimsby Institute
