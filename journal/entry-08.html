Entry VIII

The Offsite Journal of Elias Grimsby

American Tuesday

I have arrived in the Americas.

I believe I am in a town known as "Boston."

To ensure that my journey has not gone awry, I shall confirm with one of the locals.

My journey, as it seems, did not directly bring me to Boston.

I am in a small annex of Boston known as "Bah-stn."

For confirmation, I asked a young dockhand to spell it.

I believe the dockhand is hard of hearing.

I asked the same question, only louder and slower.

The dockhand continued to look confused.

I asked him how I get from Bah-stn to Boston.

The dockhand remained confused.

I became confused.

He remained confused.

I asked where I could procure transportation.

He pointed toward a window.

I compensated the dockhand for his time and approached the window.

I found that I could either take a coach or steamship.

I was warned that the coach would be a rather bumpy ride.

I chose the steamship.

Comfort is important.

More importantly, it is boarding now.

I shall report my findings from the ship.

Signed, Elias Grimsby The Grimsby Institute
