The Offsite Journal of Elias Grimsby

The First Friday Since Disembarkation, Mercifully

I have arrived in a curious town in Maine.

I am unaware of the name of it.

The name is inconsequential.

What is important is that I am no longer on that steamship.

The SS Lavatory and I have different definitions of the word "comfort."

I assume theirs has more to do with their familiarity with fecal matter rather than plush accommodations.

This has been noted.

I have passed by a group of four youths.

They seemed perplexed by my attire.

And I theirs.

After a brief conversation, I have noticed that the youths here are quite sheltered.

During our conversation, they proposed I join their search party to "find a dead body."

I replied, as a scientist, "Another one?"

The youths seemed wary of my response and continued walking.

One was exceptionally preoccupied with having a comb.

I am unsure of the significance, considering he had what the Americans would call a "buzz cut."

Either way.

Godspeed, youths.

I ventured further into town.

The town is quite lovely.

Rather mid-century in design.

I see a red car.

I have no empirical evidence of this, but...

... I feel like the red car is angry with me.

I am choosing not to make eye contact with the car.

However difficult that might be.

This town seems to have a lot going on and very little Gary.

The search continues!

Signed, Elias Grimsby The Grimsby Institute
