Entry IX

The Offsite Journal of Elias Grimsby

American Tuesday, Nautical

My goodness, this ship is pungent.

Could be the air.

Could be the other passengers.

Could be some amalgamation of all variables.

I kept my Reese's close by.

I overheard passengers discussing leaving the "Ha-bah"

A Ha-Bah?

A funny sheep perhaps?

Does America have humorous livestock?

I regret not visiting this enchanted land sooner.

I simply must return when I have more time.

I am desperate to hear a sheep tell jokes.

Onward to Maine!

Signed, Elias Grimsby The Grimsby Institute
